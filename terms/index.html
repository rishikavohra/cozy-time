IAbout Cozy Time

Cozy Time is a casual star-chart puzzle game set in a soft twilight sky. Each night, a small chart in the corner shows a constellation, and its stars are hidden in a large sky among many others that look alike. You find the right stars and connect them with golden lines; when every line on the chart is drawn, an animal painted in stardust appears.

Each of the twelve constellations has three nights. Dusk marks where to begin, Midnight turns or mirrors the sky, and Dawn hides the chart behind a cloud that you can press and hold to peek. The app also includes the night sky home screen, the Star Atlas, the Cloud Stall, the Moon Chime, Tonight's Sky, the Wish Lanterns, a How to Play page and Settings.

IVGameplay, Stardust and Fairness

A moon at the bottom of the drawing screen shows your patience. A line that is not on the chart makes it wane, and when it becomes a new moon you simply take a short rest and carry on. Ratings depend on how many lines missed the chart and how many lanterns you used, and finished nights can be drawn again at any time to try for a better rating.

Stardust is earned inside the game by finishing nights, drawing Tonight's Sky, ringing the Moon Chime and releasing Wish Lanterns. It can be exchanged at the Cloud Stall for lanterns, spyglasses, moon tea and new night skies. Stardust and helpers exist only inside Cozy Time and have no value outside the game.

The Gentle moon option in Settings makes the moon wane only on every second slip, for anyone who would like a calmer pace. It is a comfort setting, and using it is always welcome.

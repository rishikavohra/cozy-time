Cozy Time

Privacy Policy

Cozy Time is a calm game about drawing constellations: a small star chart shows the shape, and you find its stars in a busy night sky and connect them with golden lines. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app keeps while you play, why it keeps it, and the choices you have.

IWhat the App Keeps

To remember your night sky between visits, Cozy Time keeps a small set of game records. These records describe your play, and they are created only by the actions you take inside the app.

  • For each of the twelve constellations and each of its three nights (Dusk, Midnight and Dawn): whether the night is finished and the best rating you earned.
  • For a night you are still drawing: which golden lines you have already connected, how many lines missed the chart and how many lanterns helped, so you can continue later and your rating is fair.
  • Your stardust, the lanterns, spyglasses and cups of moon tea in your bag, and the night skies you have collected and chosen at the Cloud Stall.
  • Your place in the seven-night Moon Chime calendar, whether Tonight's Sky has been drawn today, and which Wish Lanterns you have released.
  • A running count of the golden lines you have drawn.
  • Your preferences from the Settings page: music, sound effects, haptics and gentle moon, plus small markers that remember which tips you have already seen.

IIHow It Is Used

Each record has one simple job. Finished nights decide which constellations glow in your sky, which night is ready next and which constellations are still hidden behind the clouds. Saved lines let you leave in the middle of a constellation and find it exactly as you left it. Ratings appear in the Star Atlas and on each constellation's page.

Your stardust and bag decide what the Cloud Stall shows as yours and how many helpers you can use while drawing. The Moon Chime calendar decides which moon phase is waiting tonight, and the line count, finished nights and chimes rung decide which Wish Lanterns are ready to release.

Your preferences decide whether background music plays, whether chimes sound when a line connects, whether your iPhone gives a gentle tap, and whether the moon of patience wanes on every slip or on every second one. These records are read when a screen opens and written back at the moment something changes, such as when a line connects or a switch is turned.

IIIWhere It Is Kept

All of the records described above are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They stay there for as long as Cozy Time is installed, so your sky looks the same when you come back tomorrow. The records are small, a handful of numbers for each constellation and a few on-or-off values for your preferences, and they serve only the purposes listed in this policy.

If you delete Cozy Time from your device, its storage is removed together with the app, and the next installation starts with a fresh, empty sky. Device backups that you create through your own iPhone settings may include the app's storage in the same way they include other apps.

IVWhen You Write to Us

The Contact us row in Settings opens an email addressed to our team. If you choose to send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message.

We use this information only to read your message and reply to you, for example to answer a question about the game or to look into something that did not work as expected. We keep the conversation only as long as it is useful for helping you, and you are welcome to ask us to delete it at any time.

To help us understand a problem, it can be useful to mention your iPhone model, the iOS version and the constellation you were drawing. Sharing these details is entirely up to you.

VLinks and System Features

A few rows in Settings hand you over to features provided by your iPhone:

  • Privacy Policy and Terms of Use open the matching page in your web browser.
  • Rate Cozy Time asks the system to show the App Store rating prompt or review page.
  • Contact us opens the system email composer or your mail app.

Once one of these screens is open, it works under the policies of the service that provides it, such as your browser, your mail app or the App Store.

VIYour Choices

You are in control of everything the app keeps:

  • Change music, sound effects, haptics and gentle moon at any time in Settings; the change takes effect straight away and is remembered the next time you open the app. Your iPhone's own volume buttons and silent switch keep working as usual.
  • Use Show the tips again in Settings to clear the tip markers and see each guide once more.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its records from your device.
  • Write to us if you would like a past email conversation removed.

VIIChanges to This Policy

If the way Cozy Time handles information changes, for example because a new feature is added, we will update this policy so that it keeps describing the app accurately. The updated version will be published on this page, and the main points will be easy to find in the sections above.
